🌐 Staying Safe on Different Websites

📱 Social Media

Never share your home address, phone number or private information with strangers.

Tip: Keep your account private and only accept people you know.

🎮 Gaming Websites

Be careful when chatting with strangers or clicking links sent by other players.

Tip: Never share your password or personal details while gaming.

🛍️ Shopping Websites

Some shopping websites are fake and may try to steal your money or information.

Tip: Use trusted websites and ask a trusted adult before buying.

🔎 Search Engines

Not every search result is safe. Some websites contain misleading information.

Tip: Check website addresses and avoid suspicious links.

📺 Video Websites

Some videos and comments may contain inappropriate content or suspicious links.

Tip: Use safety settings and report inappropriate content.

📧 Email Websites

Scammers may send fake emails pretending to be companies or people you trust.

Tip: Don't open unexpected attachments or click suspicious links.

🔐 How to Make a Strong Password

A strong password helps protect your online accounts.

  • Use at least 12 characters.
  • Mix uppercase and lowercase letters.
  • Include numbers and symbols.
  • Never share your password with friends.
  • Use a different password for each account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication when available.
⚠️ Never use your name, birthday or simple passwords like "123456"!

🚨 How to Spot Online Scams

Fake Giveaways

Messages saying you won a prize you never entered may be scams.

Suspicious Links

Unknown links can lead to fake websites that try to steal information.

Too Good to Be True

Be careful of websites promising free money, expensive gifts or unbelievable deals.

Remember: If something feels suspicious, stop, leave the website and tell a trusted adult.
